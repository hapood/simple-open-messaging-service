SOMS — Simple Open Messaging Service

An open-source, self-hosted, federated chat platform for web and native devices. Independently operated servers communicate through the SOMFP protocol, without a central authority.

  • Cross-server friendships, direct messages, and group conversations.
  • Images, files, voice messages, and WebRTC voice and video calls.
  • Self-hosted server, web client, and administration console.

This playground uses in-memory demo data. It does not connect to a real chat server, and reloading resets the demo.
