# Group announcement: client presentation and acknowledgement

The canonical federation contract is [Group Meta](../../spec/v1/group-meta.md). This document specifies the local client and read-watermark behavior; the acknowledgement is not a federated fact.

## Coordinates and storage

A group has one current announcement projection: `(feedId, text)`. A positive `feedId` with `text: null` is an explicit clear. An absent projection is not an explicit clear. The announcement `feedId` belongs to the canonical Group Meta feed, not the message-sequence space. The origin allocates a new coordinate for each effective publish, edit, or clear. Both the origin and a mirror persist the projection; mirrors never author it. A full recovery commits its roster, announcement and cursor together. A completed full recovery replaces the announcement projection with the one returned by the complete scan, or removes the previous projection if none was returned. An announcement returned by an earlier page is applied even if the origin later replaces it above the fixed `through` boundary; subsequent delta synchronization applies the update. An incomplete recovery does not replace any committed announcement.

Each local account's `AccountThreadState.announcementReadFeedId` is the largest announcement coordinate it has actually viewed for that group. It is not `lastReadSeq`, `groupMetaFeedEndId`, or a peer's read position. Acknowledge a displayed coordinate using `POST /group/announcement/read`; the operation is idempotent, monotone, and constrained to a known group coordinate. A delayed acknowledgement of an older announcement must not mark a newer one as viewed. The account thread-feed snapshot carries the watermark across the user's devices.

## Wake and visibility

An origin mutation commits its announcement projection and the existing durable client/federation thread-head fan-out together. Remote peers drain Group Meta through the usual confirmed-audience path; local clients drain their own Group Meta feed. The existing `THREAD_FEED_UPDATED { threadFeedEndId }` SSE event is only a wake-up hint. It carries neither announcement text nor a separate announcement coordinate, and losing it cannot prevent recovery by cursor pull.

The client may automatically open the chatbox-scoped announcement popup only for a foreground, active group when its committed current announcement is known and non-null, its Group Meta scan cursor has reached the last server-advertised Group Meta head, and its feedId exceeds the account's read watermark. A cached announcement during an unresolved full recovery or pending Group Meta drain is not sufficient. Opening a popup records its displayed `feedId`; acknowledgement uses exactly that coordinate, including if another edit arrives before dismissal. The title-side loudspeaker opens the known non-null announcement on demand regardless of watermark. Plain text must not be interpreted as HTML. Group information (opened from the chat header's More button) contains the announcement management entry. Only local OWNER/ADMIN users may publish or clear; other current members can read. Clearing does not create a message or a read alert.
